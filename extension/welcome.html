Scam Shield checks every site you open against 700,000+ known scam and malware sites, updated every 5 minutes, and blocks fake pages that try to steal your card.

First: allow on-screen warnings

Scam Shield can pop up a notification on your screen when a page isn't secure (no encryption), looks like a scam, or when you're about to type a password or card number where you shouldn't.

Your browser will ask you to confirm. You can turn this off any time in the Scam Shield popup.

1. Tell us your bank

Click the Scam Shield icon in your toolbar → My bank(s). Only your bank's real website will be trusted with your card; look-alikes get blocked.

2. New account? You're a target — here's why

Scammers buy lists of new customers and high-balance accounts from data leaks. Expect fake "activate your new card", "verify your account" or "pending e-Transfer" messages in your first months.

  • Your bank will never ask for your PIN, CVV, full card number, or a one-time code by text, e-mail or phone.
  • Never click links in messages about your account — open your bank's app or type its address.
  • Turn on transaction alerts and 2-step verification in your banking app today.
  • If someone calls "from your bank", hang up and call the number on the back of your card.

3. Check anything suspicious

Paste a message into the Scam Shield popup, or select text / right-click a link → Check with Scam Shield. Dangerous links are reported automatically so they get taken down for everyone.
