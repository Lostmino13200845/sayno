HELP & FAQ

Short answers in plain language. Select a question to open it.

What is SAYNO?
SAYNO helps you spot scams before you give away sensitive information. It checks suspicious messages and websites, and Card Guard protects you when a page asks for your card details.
What does HIGH RISK mean?
SAYNO found several signs that scammers commonly use. It is a strong warning, not a certainty. Do not click the link, do not reply and do not enter any details.
Can I trust a SAFE result?
SAFE means SAYNO did not find strong scam signs. It does not guarantee that a message or website is trustworthy. If something still feels wrong, contact the company using an app or phone number you already trust.
Does SAYNO store my messages?
No. Your message is checked on your device. It is not uploaded to SAYNO and not stored.
What does Card Guard protect?
Card Guard watches for pages that ask for your card number, security code or PIN. If the page is not one of your banks or a known payment provider, it warns you or blocks the page before you type.
What happens when a page is blocked?
SAYNO covers the page with a clear message that says why. Choose "Go back to safety" to leave the page. If you stopped before entering anything, you did not enter or send anything.
What should I do if I think I entered my card information?
Call your bank right away, using the number on the back of your card, and ask them to block or replace the card. Check your statements closely over the next weeks. You can also report the scam to the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre.
How do I install the extension?
Follow the steps on the Get the extension page. The message and website checks on this website work without installing anything.
Does SAYNO work on my phone?
The message and website checks work in your phone's browser. Card Guard and the page rating are part of the desktop browser extension.
