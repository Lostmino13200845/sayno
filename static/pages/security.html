Security

How SAYNO keeps you protected.

A short, plain-language look at what is happening behind each warning.

Local detection

Messages, links and card fields are checked inside your browser or on this website, on your device. They are not sent to SAYNO to be checked.

Threat intelligence

SAYNO compares what you check with public lists of known dangerous websites: URLhaus, ThreatFox, OpenPhish, Phishing Army, CERT Polska and Phishing.Database. The lists are refreshed every few hours.

Browser protection

The browser extension rates the pages you open and shows the result on its icon. Card Guard warns or blocks a page that asks for card details when it is not one of your banks or a known payment provider.

Sensitive-data handling

There are no accounts, and SAYNO does not store what you check. Card details you type stay on your device and are never sent anywhere.

Responsible reporting

SAYNO never reports anything on your behalf. When a result is risky it points you to places where you can report a scam yourself, such as the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre.

Open code

SAYNO's code is public on GitHub, so anyone can read how it works. Every change is tested automatically on Linux, macOS and Windows.
