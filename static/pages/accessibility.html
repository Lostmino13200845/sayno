Accessibility

Built to be used by everyone.

SAYNO is designed so that warnings are easy to find, read and act on, whatever you use to browse.

  • Keyboard accessibility

    Every link, button and field can be reached and used with the keyboard, in a sensible order, with a clearly visible focus outline.

  • Clear labels

    Every field and button says what it is. Icons that stand alone, such as search, have a text label for screen readers.

  • Readable hierarchy

    Each page has one main heading, short sections and plain language. The most important thing is always the biggest.

  • Risk never depends on colour

    HIGH RISK, BE CAREFUL and SAFE are written out, use different shapes and icons, and come with an explanation, so they are clear without seeing colour.

  • Accessible controls

    Buttons and links are large enough to tap or click easily, and text keeps strong contrast against its background in light and dark mode.

  • Clear errors and warnings

    Messages say what happened and what to do next, in everyday words. Results and warnings are announced to screen readers when they appear.
