About SAYNO

A safer way to navigate suspicious messages and websites.

The problem

Scam messages are built to look real and to rush you. People who are new to a country's banks have no way to know what a real message looks like, so "your card is locked, verify now" is easy to believe.

The approach

Check, understand, act. SAYNO gives a clear answer in plain language, explains the reasons, and makes the safest next step the biggest one on the screen.

How protection works

One protection layer with three checks: the message, the website, and Card Guard. All of it runs on your device.
