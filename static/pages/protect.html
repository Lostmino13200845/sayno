Has my password appeared in a data breach?

Your password is hashed (SHA-1) in this browser and only the first 5 characters of the hash are sent to Have I Been Pwned, which answers with every hash that starts the same way. Your browser does the final comparison, so the password itself never leaves your device.

To see whether your email address was in a breach, visit haveibeenpwned.com directly.
