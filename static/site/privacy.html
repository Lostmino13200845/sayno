Privacy policy

Applies to the SAYNO browser extension and the SAYNO server it talks to. Last updated 4 October 2026.

The short version

  • The pages you visit stay on your device. The extension rates each page itself. It does not send your browsing history, page contents or card numbers to us.
  • The server hears from the extension in three cases, listed below. In each, it receives the least it needs.
  • We do not sell data, show ads or build profiles.

What the extension does on your device

For every web page you open, the extension checks the address locally: it looks for warning signs in the link (look-alike names, disguised addresses, risky endings and similar) and compares a hash of the address with a list of known-bad fingerprints stored on your device. If the page asks for card details, the extension decides locally whether to block it. Card numbers you type are never read out of the page and never leave your device.

What is stored on your device

Your settings (for example your chosen bank), the block-list fingerprints, and the rating of each open tab for the current browser session. You can remove all of it by uninstalling the extension.

What we do not collect

  • Browsing history, or a list of the sites you visit.
  • Page contents, form entries, passwords or card numbers.
  • Your name, email address or any account (there are no accounts).

Where the block lists come from

Public threat feeds: URLhaus and ThreatFox (abuse.ch), OpenPhish, Phishing Army, CERT Polska and the Phishing.Database project. The server refreshes them every few minutes.

Security

All traffic to the SAYNO server uses HTTPS. The public server keeps no scan history and has no administrator page. Requests are rate-limited to protect it from abuse.

Children

SAYNO does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
