Scam Shield Design System

Every screen of the website, the web scanner and the browser extension is built from the tokens and components on this page. Change a token in design/tokens.css, run py tools/sync_design.py, and it changes everywhere. Use the button bottom-right to check dark mode.

Colours

Brand, surfaces and the risk scale. The risk scale is the core of the product: it must stay readable for colour-blind users, which is why every level also carries a word or symbol (SAFE, !, !!, ✖).

🛡️ Stop: this page wants your card details

Scam Shield blocked this page on rbc-card-activation.help because:

  • It asks for your card PIN. No bank or shop ever asks for your PIN on a website.
  • It uses bank-style pressure but this is not your bank's website.

Your bank will never ask for your full card number, CVV, PIN or one-time code on a page you reached from a link.
