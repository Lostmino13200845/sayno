How it works

Three layers between you and a scammer

🔎

Checks every site

Every page you open is compared with 700,000+ known scam sites from global security teams, plus 50+ look-alike tricks like rbc-card-activation.help. The shield on your toolbar changes colour.

💳

Card Guard

If a page asks for your card PIN, or pretends to be your bank while asking for card details, Card Guard blocks it before you type. Your card number is never sent anywhere.

🚩

Reports it for everyone

Dangerous links are reported automatically to the security network that browsers and antivirus products use, so the scam gets blocked and taken down for other people too.

    Every option explained

    What each setting does

    Block fake pages that ask for my card

    What it does: Card Guard. It watches for card number, CVV, expiry, PIN and SIN fields, and for card numbers being typed. It blocks the page if it asks for a PIN, pretends to be a bank on a non-bank site, is rated risky, or isn't encrypted.

    Default: onTurn off: only if a trusted site is wrongly blocked (tell us!)

    New-account mode

    What it does: adds gentle reminders whenever you type a card anywhere, and stricter checks on pages that talk about banking. Made for your first months with a new account, or when you hold a large balance.

    Default: onTurn off: once you know your bank's real messages well

    Notify me on screen when a page isn't secure

    What it does: pops up a notification on your screen when a page has no encryption (http), when Scam Shield rates it risky, or when you're about to type a password or card number on an unencrypted page. Buttons let you leave the page in one click.

    Asks your permission first (on install, or when you switch it on)At most one alert per site every 30 min

    🏦 My bank(s)

    What it does: tells Scam Shield which bank websites are really yours. Only those, plus big payment pages like Stripe or PayPal, are trusted with your card. Look-alikes such as rbc-secure-login.xyz are not.

    Default: all major Canadian banks trustedTip: tick only your own bank

    📋 Check a message

    What it does: paste any text, e-mail or link into the popup to get a danger score from 0 to 100 and the reasons behind it. Pasting checks it instantly, with no button needed.

    Where: toolbar shield → popupAlso: right-click selected text or a link

    🟢 Toolbar badge

    What it does: the shield shows how risky the current site is. No badge means nothing was found; ! means be careful; !! means high risk; ✖ means a known scam; off means the server can't be reached.

    Always on

    🚩 Automatic reporting

    What it does: when a link is clearly dangerous, Scam Shield sends it (only the link, never your personal data) to Netcraft's takedown network, so browsers and antivirus products block it for everyone.

    Default: onNever reported: your card data, your messages, local addresses

    Install

    Up and running in 3 steps

    1. 1

      Start Scam Shield

      Double-click start.bat in the Scam Shield folder. Keep that window open.

    2. 2

      Add the extension

      Open edge://extensions or chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and choose the extension folder.

    3. 3

      Pin the shield & pick your bank

      Click the puzzle icon → pin Scam Shield. Open it and tick your bank under My bank(s).

    No extension? Use the web scanner to check any message, and the password breach check.

    Stay safe

    Your bank will never…

    ask for your PIN, CVV or one-time code by text, e-mail or phone
    send a link asking you to “activate” or “unlock” your card
    ask you to deposit an e-Transfer through a link and your card details
    pressure you to act within hours or lose your account
    ask you to move money to a “safe account”
    be offended if you hang up and call the number on your card
