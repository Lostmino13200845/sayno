        Data-breach prevention checklist

        Tick items as you complete them — your progress is saved in this browser.

        🚨 If you were hit (clicked, typed a password, paid, or gave a code)

        1. Money involved? Call your bank/card company immediately using the number on the back of your card. Ask them to block the card and dispute the payment.
        2. Disconnect the affected device from the internet if you installed anything or gave remote access. Uninstall AnyDesk/TeamViewer etc.
        3. From a different, clean device: change the password of the affected account, then every account that used the same password.
        4. Sign out all sessions and remove unknown devices (Google, Microsoft, Apple, Facebook all have "sign out everywhere").
        5. Turn on 2-step verification and check recovery e-mail / phone number weren't changed. Check e-mail forwarding rules.
        6. Scan the device — Windows Security → Virus & threat protection → Scan options → Microsoft Defender Offline scan.
        7. Identity data leaked (ID, SSN, date of birth)? Freeze your credit with the credit bureaus and set up fraud alerts.
        8. Keep evidence (screenshots, the message, transaction IDs) and report to police / your national fraud agency (links appear under a suspicious scan result).
        9. Watch bank statements and your accounts closely for the next months. Beware of "recovery" scammers who promise to get your money back for a fee.
